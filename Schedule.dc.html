Dress code

Western business attire

A suit or equivalent formal business dress. Jeans, trainers and hoodies do not count, and neither does a blazer over a t-shirt. National dress is always welcome and always appropriate. The thing people get wrong is footwear: you will be on your feet for most of an unmoderated caucus, so wear shoes you can stand in for two full days.

Conduct

How we expect you to behave

Attack the argument, never the delegate — in committee, at lunch, and online afterwards. Phones stay away during formal debate. Position papers must be your own work; anything copied or generated wholesale is disqualified from awards. A delegate who crosses a line gets one warning from the dais, and after that the Secretariat is involved and their school is told.

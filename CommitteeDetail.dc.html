About the committee

The Security Council is the only UN body whose decisions member states are legally obliged to carry out. It can impose sanctions, authorise peacekeeping deployments, refer situations to the International Criminal Court, and sanction the use of force. In this room you hold those powers for real: a resolution that passes here binds every state in the simulation, and a single permanent member can stop it.

Debate here is faster and more confrontational than in a general assembly committee, and the veto shapes everything: there is no point drafting language the permanent five will never accept. Expect frequent unmoderated caucus and late-breaking crisis updates. The chairs are looking for delegates who negotiate text line by line rather than delivering speeches, and who can concede a clause to save a resolution.

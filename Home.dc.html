Welcome

Nobody here has done this before either

You will walk into a room of people you have never met, holding a country you did not choose, and someone will ask you to speak first. That moment is the whole point. Everything that follows — the notes, the blocs, the resolution that nearly collapses at the vote — grows out of it.

This is the first ISMUN, so none of it is a tradition yet. The procedure will be real, the topics current, and the chairs properly prepared. But the character of this conference is still unwritten, and the delegates in the first room are the ones who get to write it.

The Basics

What is ISMUN?

You take a country, you take a crisis, and you argue your way to something the room can live with.

Real procedure, properly run

Genuine UN rules of procedure, real draft resolutions, and chairs who will hold you to both. Nothing is watered down because you are new to it.

Issues that are actually live

Topics taken from this year's headlines rather than a textbook, so no one spends a weekend re-arguing a question the world already settled.

Skills that outlast the weekend

Negotiating, drafting, and speaking to a room that disagrees with you — three things you will keep using long after the final gavel.
